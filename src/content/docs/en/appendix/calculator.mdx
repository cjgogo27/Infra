## Explain what changes

Increase concurrency from 1 to 8. Weights stay unchanged—what happens to KV memory? Reduce the optimized stage's share from 40% to 10%. How much does a twofold local speedup still help?

Training-state estimates use the explicit 16-byte assumption in Section 1.3. KV estimates use the standard Transformer formula in Section 5.1. Sharding, alternative architectures, quantization, and windowing require adjustments.
